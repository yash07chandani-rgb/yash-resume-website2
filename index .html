A curiosity for how business works.

My interest in business started early. In Class 5, I informally explored selling pens to my classmates, which gave me an early experience of buying and selling. Over time, that curiosity grew into a deeper interest in finance, business analysis and entrepreneurship.

Today, I am pursuing B.Com (Hons) at Sri Guru Gobind Singh College of Commerce, University of Delhi, alongside the BBA in Digital Business and Entrepreneurship programme at IIM Bangalore. I enjoy stepping outside my comfort zone, exploring new ideas and turning them into practical projects.
